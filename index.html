About the Lab

Understanding How the Brain Integrates Information to Generate Behavior

The Praful S. Singru Lab focuses on understanding how the brain integrates hormonal, sensory, and environmental information to generate complex behaviors and maintain physiological homeostasis.

Our work lies at the intersection of behavioral neuroscience, neuroendocrinology, hypothalamic circuitry, social and maternal behavior, energy balance, and neuropeptide signaling. A major focus is the role of hypothalamic neural circuits in orchestrating adaptive behavioral responses such as maternal aggression, feeding, stress adaptation, and defensive behavior.

The laboratory combines neuroanatomical mapping, immunohistochemistry, molecular neuroscience, and behavioral assays to dissect the mechanisms through which neuropeptides and hypothalamic nuclei regulate behavior.
