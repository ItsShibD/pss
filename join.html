A Note from the PI

We Welcome Motivated Researchers

The Singru Lab is an intellectually ambitious, collaborative, and supportive environment. We believe the best science happens when people with diverse perspectives and complementary skills pursue a shared set of questions with rigor and creativity.

Our work sits at the interface of neuroanatomy, molecular biology, physiology, and behavior — so there is always something to learn and something new to discover. Whether you are an experienced researcher or a student taking your first steps in a laboratory, we are committed to your growth and success.

I encourage prospective students to read our recent publications and to write with a thoughtful statement of your research interests and why our lab is the right fit for you.

Dr. Praful S. Singru
Associate Professor · NISER Bhubaneswar

Expectations

What We Look For

Intellectual Curiosity

A genuine desire to understand how the brain works. Familiarity with neuroscience is helpful but not required — enthusiasm and willingness to learn matter most.

Scientific Rigor

Commitment to careful, reproducible experimentation. We place high value on attention to detail, sound experimental design, and honest interpretation of data.

Collaborative Spirit

Science is a team effort. Lab members participate actively in group meetings, help each other troubleshoot, and contribute to a positive and inclusive lab culture.

Initiative

We mentor actively, but increasingly encourage students to take ownership of their projects, read broadly, and propose new directions as they develop.

Campus Life

About NISER Bhubaneswar

NISER (National Institute of Science Education and Research) is an autonomous institution of national importance established by the Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India. One of India's premier research universities, it offers an intellectually vibrant campus with state-of-the-art research infrastructure.

PhD students at NISER receive a competitive fellowship, on-campus accommodation, and health insurance in accordance with DAE/UGC norms. The institute provides a central animal facility, confocal imaging suites, flow cytometry, genome sequencing platforms, and an extensive digital library.

Bhubaneswar is a vibrant, rapidly developing city with excellent connectivity, infrastructure, and rich cultural heritage. NISER's green campus in Jatni offers a peaceful academic environment approximately 35 km from the city center.
