Dr. Praful S. Singru

Associate Professor  ·  School of Biological Sciences, NISER

Dr. Praful S. Singru is a neuroscientist and faculty member at the School of Biological Sciences, NISER Bhubaneswar. He joined NISER in 2011 and leads the Neural Circuits & Behaviour Lab.

Dr. Singru completed his PhD from Nagpur University in 2004 and subsequently worked as a Research Associate at Tufts Medical Center, Boston, before joining NISER.
